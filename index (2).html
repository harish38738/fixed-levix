Add Levix to Home Screen 📱
Follow these steps in Safari
1

Look for the ⎙ icon at the bottom of Safari (the box with an arrow pointing up)

Tap "Add to Home Screen"
Scroll down in the Share sheet to find this option — it has a + icon
3
Tap "Add" in the top right
Levix will appear on your Home Screen like a native app — no App Store needed
⚠️ Only works in Safari, not Chrome or Firefox on iOS
